Why Did I build Z-Plan ?

I created PlanZ because I wanted a simple place where I could organize the things that actually matter in my day — tasks, notes, and routines — without feeling overwhelmed by complicated productivity tools. While building it, I also wanted to challenge myself as a developer. PlanZ gave me a way to practice JavaScript, improve my UI design, work with data and local storage, and learn how to structure a real project as it grows. It started as a simple idea: make staying organized feel easier.
